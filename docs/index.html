About Bejirond

Bejirond (በጅሮንድ) is an offline-first expense manager built by TeredaTrades for people who run more than one business and want to actually keep track of the money — without handing that data to a server somewhere.

It's built in the languages people in Ethiopia and East Africa actually think in, with the Ethiopian calendar, Birr-based exchange rates, and local payment methods — Telebirr, CBE Birr, Coopay, M-Pesa — built in from day one, not bolted on later.

Frequently asked questions

Is Bejirond free to use?

Yes. Bejirond is completely free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Bejirond works fully offline — every book, entry, and setting is stored only on your phone.

Is my financial data safe?

Your data never leaves your device. There's no account, no server, and nothing to sync — Bejirond can't share what it never has.

Why isn't Bejirond on the Google Play Store yet?

It hasn't been submitted there yet. Until then, this page is the official way to get it — the app is built automatically from public source code, and the download link above always points to the newest version.

Which languages does Bejirond support?

English, Amharic, Afaan Oromoo, Tigrinya, French, Arabic, and Swahili. Switch anytime from Settings.

Can I track more than one business?

Yes. Bejirond keeps a separate book, team, and set of reports for each business you add.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Open an issue on the GitHub repository, or use the review form above — both reach the person building it directly.
